Delete your BizPOS account

You can delete your account and its data at any time.

Shop owners (BizPOS app)

  1. Open BizPOS and go to More > Account.
  2. Tap Delete my account, enter your password and type DELETE.

This deletes your sign-in, company record, online store and its products. Data on the phone is erased if you choose so. Payment records may be kept where the law requires.

Shoppers (website account)

Sign in and delete your shopper account, coins and profile. Orders you placed stay with the shops for their records.

Can't sign in?
