Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

BizPOS is a point of sale and shop management app for Android and iOS, with an online marketplace website. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and the choices you have.

1. Data that stays on your phone

Your sales, products, stock, customers, suppliers, expenses, staff and reports are saved in a database on your phone. We do not receive this data unless you turn on a cloud feature described below.

2. Data we collect when you create an account

  • Account: your name, email address and sign-in method (email/password or Google), used to sign you in and recover your account.
  • Shop record: shop name, business type, city, phone number, your plan and the phones signed in to the shop, used to run subscriptions and support.
  • Usage totals: number of products, customers and suppliers, and monthly sales totals, so BizPOS support can help you and manage plans. Individual sales and customer details are not sent.

3. Online store and orders

  • If you open an online store, the products you choose (name, price, category, stock status and photos), your shop name, phone, address, city and store location are shown publicly on the BizPOS website.
  • When a customer orders, their name, phone number, delivery address and note are sent to the shop that receives the order.
  • Website shoppers who create an account give their name, email, phone and address, and earn BizPOS coins. They can see their own orders.

4. Phone permissions

  • Camera: scanning barcodes and taking product photos.
  • Microphone: adding stock by voice, only while you use that feature.
  • Location: setting your store's location for the "nearby stores" list, only when you tap it. Shoppers' location is used in the browser to sort stores by distance and is not stored.
  • Bluetooth: printing to receipt printers.
  • Fingerprint / face: unlocking the app. Your fingerprint never leaves the phone; Android or iOS checks it.
  • Notifications: low stock, reminders and new online orders.

5. Google Drive backup

If you turn on Google Drive backup, your shop data is saved in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. BizPOS cannot read it.

6. Services we use

Google Firebase (sign-in and cloud database) and Google Drive. These providers process data for us under their own privacy terms. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

7. Keeping and deleting data

We keep account data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app (More > Account > Delete my account) or by following the steps on our account deletion page. Payment records needed for accounting may be kept as required by law.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Access to cloud data is limited by security rules so that each shop sees only its own data. The app supports a PIN, fingerprint and two-factor codes for the owner and staff.

9. Children

BizPOS is made for businesses and is not directed at children under 13.
